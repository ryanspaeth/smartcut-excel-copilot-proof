What this connector is

The connector tests native Excel Copilot against three fictional invoices. It reads no PDFs and no customer documents. It does not modify Excel and it does not create a genuine SmartCut workbook snip. Access mode is synthetic_no_auth. Microsoft identity and paid SmartCut licensing are not verified. All callers share one server-generated synthetic run.

Tool access is limited to at most 100 searches and 100 new records, from 4 October 2026 20:10 Europe/Berlin until 5 October 2026 20:10 Europe/Berlin. Infrastructure may remain deployed after tool access ends.

Data the connector receives

Tool inputs you or Copilot send to the endpoint reach the proof server in the isolated Firebase project smartcut-copilot-proof-893c5f (region europe-west3). Those inputs are expected to be fabricated prompts and identifiers for the disposable Evidence workbook only (INV-SYN-001, INV-SYN-002, INV-SYN-003 and their configured targets).

The connector does not ask for your Microsoft account, does not verify it, and does not receive a SmartCut license. Tenant staging does not restrict this anonymous public endpoint.

What is stored

Proof searches and simulated snip records persist in that isolated Firebase project for the life of the synthetic run. Searches expire after ten minutes, or earlier when the anonymous run expires. A simulated record is written only after fetched, unambiguous fictional evidence, and identical retries return the existing record. Status of those records is simulated.

Logs exclude query contents and bearer tokens. Infrastructure can retain request metadata (for example time, status, and routing data).

What we do not do

This pilot does not sell personal data, does not use inputs to train a model, and does not read customer files. Do not send real invoices, customer documents, or personal data. If you do, contact office@smart-cut.io and we will delete what the proof store still holds, to the extent the run has not already expired.

Legal basis and retention

Processing is limited to operating this short technical proof at your request when you invoke the connector. Stored proof records and searches are kept only until the run expires (5 October 2026 20:10 Europe/Berlin) unless an earlier deletion is requested. Request metadata kept by Google Cloud may follow that platform’s normal log retention.
